01 // Core Gameplay

Four Pillars of Shop Mastery

Every system inside Gaming Lounge Simulator is engineered around responsive retail decisions—from ordering hot releases at wholesale discounts to expanding your arcade lounge and staff roster.

PC Simulation First-Person Retail & Arcade Unity 6
PILLAR 01 600+ Titles · 12 Genres

Stock Parody Game Cases & Complete Your Codex

Order physical game cases across twelve distinct genres—from Call of Doody and Door Infernal to The Snitcher 3, President Evil, and Dark Bowls—each with custom front, spine, and back cover art.

  • 12 genre aisles with dedicated shelf signs & mixed bargain displays
  • Daily wholesale supplier rotation & featured New Release hype drops
  • Collectible in-game Codex tracking discovered covers, ratings, and pricing
PILLAR 02 3-Layer Demand Engine

Read a Living Market & 28-Day Season Calendar

Customer preferences shift dynamically every day across three interacting simulation layers: procedural market curves, yearly seasonal calendar events, and fresh release hype windows.

  • Interactive Trends forecast app projecting demand windows 2–14 days out
  • 28-Day Season Calendar showing upcoming genre modifiers & drops
  • Autonomous NPC customers with category demand budgets & shelf-search AI
PILLAR 03 Cozy Storefront & Arcade

Customize Every Finish & Add Playable Arcades

Design a bright, welcoming store from sunrise to late-night rush. Order new furniture on your tablet, customize shop finishes, expand your floor footprint, and install playable arcade cabinets.

  • Modular 3-, 4-, and 5-row shelves, display racks, and interior styling options
  • Dynamic time-of-day lighting with warm street lamps and cozy atmosphere
  • Arcade lounge cabinets that attract browsing customers and generate extra revenue
PILLAR 04 Staff Book & 75 Skills

Hire Staff & Master the 75-Node Skill Tree

Scale from solo shopkeeper to full retail manager. Recruit cashiers and shelf organizers from daily applicant pools and invest profits into six deep progression branches.

  • Staff Book with Cashier & Organization speed tiers (Bad to Excellent) & daily payroll
  • 6 Skill Branches: Operations, Customer Experience, Daily Suppliers, New Releases, Staff, Arcade
  • Unlocks larger carry capacity, bulk discounts, bonus copies, and longer shop days
